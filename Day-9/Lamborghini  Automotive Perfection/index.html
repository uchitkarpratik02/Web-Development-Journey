Founded in 1963 by Ferruccio Lamborghini, the brand represents extreme performance, iconic sharp-edged design, and uncompromising engineering built in Sant'Agata Bolognese, Italy.

Iconic Lineup

Miura

1966 – 1973

Widely considered the world's first true supercar, featuring a revolutionary mid-engine V12 layout that set the standard for high-performance vehicles.

Countach

1974 – 1990

Pioneered the aggressive wedge shape design and iconic scissor doors that became the signature visual trait of flagship V12 Lamborghinis.

Aventador

2011 – 2022

Defined the modern era with a full carbon-fiber monocoque chassis and a naturally aspirated 6.5-liter V12 engine.
