About Lawn Care

A healthy lawn needs proper watering, mowing, fertilizing, sunlight, and regular maintenance. With the right care, your lawn can remain green and attractive all year long.

Essential Lawn Care

💧

Watering

Water your lawn regularly, preferably in the morning, to keep the grass healthy and hydrated.

✂️

Mowing

Mow your lawn regularly and avoid cutting more than one-third of the grass height at a time.

🌱

Fertilizing

Use suitable fertilizer to provide essential nutrients and encourage strong grass growth.

☀️

Sunlight

Make sure your grass receives enough sunlight for healthy and consistent growth.

Lawn Care Tips

  • Water deeply rather than watering lightly every day.
  • Keep your lawn mower blades sharp.
  • Remove weeds regularly.
  • Aerate compacted soil when necessary.
  • Use fertilizer according to your grass type and soil needs.
