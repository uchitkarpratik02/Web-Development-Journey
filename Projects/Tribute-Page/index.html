Mahatma Gandhi

The Father of the Nation (India)

"Be the change that you wish to see in the world."

Timeline of a Legacy

  • 1869 - Born in Porbandar, Gujarat, India.
  • 1888 - Traveled to London to study law.
  • 1893 - Moved to South Africa, where he spent 21 years fighting discrimination.
  • 1915 - Returned to India and joined the Indian National Congress.
  • 1930 - Led the 241-mile Dandi Salt March to protest the British salt monopoly.
  • 1942 - Launched the Quit India Movement demanding complete independence.
  • 1947 - India achieved independence from British rule.
  • 1948 - Assassinated in New Delhi at the age of 78.
