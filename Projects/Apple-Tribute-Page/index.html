The Legacy

Founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple Inc. transformed computing from a complex hobby into an intuitive experience. Through relentless pursuit of simplicity, minimalist design, and hardware-software integration, Apple changed personal technology forever.

Milestones of Innovation

1977

Apple II

One of the first highly successful mass-produced microcomputer products, featuring color graphics.

1984

Macintosh

Introduced the graphical user interface (GUI) and mouse to the masses, revolutionizing personal computing.

2001

iPod

"1,000 songs in your pocket." Transformed the entire music industry and consumer electronics.

2007

iPhone

Reinvented the phone by combining a touch device, iPod, and mobile communicator into one seamless package.

2020

Apple Silicon (M1)

Shifted Mac processors in-house, setting new industry benchmarks for performance and efficiency.
